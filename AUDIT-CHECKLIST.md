# Site audit and starter checklist

Audit date: 25 September 2026. Benchmarked against eight comparable solo
executive-coach sites (Amelia Kruse, Gina Restani, David Boulos, Brooke Taylor,
Stacey Ashley, Jennifer Minturn / Hot Lava, Binod Shankar, Dr Corrie Block).

Branding, colours, and tone of voice are being co-prepared separately and are
not covered here. This list is everything else.

Legend: [ ] to do, [x] done. "Needs from Megha" = an input only she can give.

- [ ] Add a LinkedIn link. Every reference site has one; this site has none. Needs from Megha: LinkedIn profile URL.

## 2. Trust and proof (the biggest gap vs all eight sites)
- [ ] Testimonials: 3 to 5 real client quotes with name, title, company (or anonymised title only, common in the Gulf). Needs from Megha: reach out to past clients; collect quotes and permission.
- [ ] Move the "career built across" logo strip (Traveazy, Barclays, Citibank) higher on the page, near the hero. UAE sites lead with credentials.
- [ ] Add the ICF mark next to the CPCC badge once ACC is granted. Needs from Megha: ACC status and date.
- [ ] Numbers strip: years of experience, countries, people led, leaders coached. Needs from Megha: the figures she is comfortable claiming.
- [ ] Press, podcasts, speaking: any mentions to show as a media strip. Needs from Megha: list of appearances, or a note that there are none yet.

## 3. Offer and conversion
- [ ] Decide on pricing display for the Six-Session Journey: show the price, show "from", or keep it enquiry-only. Needs from Megha: price and preference.
- [ ] Add a corporate or team offering (team coaching, leadership workshops, CPO advisory). Six of the eight sites sell to organisations as well as individuals. Needs from Megha: whether she wants corporate work and what it looks like.
- [ ] Replace the "share payment details" email link with Stripe checkout or a Payment Link. Needs from Megha: Stripe account in her name.
- [ ] Add a "Who this is for / who this is not for" block. Sharpens fit before the call.
- [ ] A softer first step than booking a call: a short guide, a self-assessment, or a newsletter signup. Needs from Megha: what she would be happy to give away.

## 4. Content and search
- [ ] Give each article its own page and URL. Today all seven articles are folded inside the homepage, so they cannot be shared or found on Google.
- [ ] Add social sharing tags (Open Graph and Twitter) with a preview image so links shared on LinkedIn and WhatsApp show a card.
- [ ] Add structured data (Person and LocalBusiness) so Google understands this is a Dubai coaching business.
- [ ] Google Business Profile for WithinWorks Leadership, Dubai. Needs from Megha: verify ownership (postcard or phone).
- [ ] Decide on keywords to target ("executive coach Dubai", "leadership coach UAE", "career transition coach"). The UAE reference sites rank on exactly these.
- [ ] Publishing rhythm for the articles in content-drafts (one a month is enough). Needs from Megha: commitment and topics.

## 5. Technical and legal
- [ ] Analytics (Vercel Analytics or Plausible). Currently nothing is tracked.
- [ ] Privacy policy and terms page. Required once Stripe and analytics are live.
- [ ] Contact form that actually submits (currently a mailto link). Use Formspree or a Vercel function so submissions are reliable and logged.
- [ ] Compress the CPCC badge image (126 KB, should be under 30 KB) and serve WebP versions of photos.
- [ ] Add a PNG favicon and Apple touch icon alongside the SVG.
- [ ] Mobile pass: check every section on a phone, especially the FAQ and article expanders.
- [ ] Accessibility pass: colour contrast, focus states, alt text on all images.
