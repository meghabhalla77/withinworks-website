About

Twenty‑five years in the room, now beside you in it.

For more than twenty‑five years I built a career across the UAE, the Middle East, and Asia, fourteen of those years inside global banks like Barclays and Citibank. Later I joined Traveazy Group as a founding team member and its Chief People Officer, building the people function from nothing and staying through the parts that tested it: growth to more than 350 employees, a pandemic, two mergers.

That path gave me real fluency in people strategy, organizational development, and cross‑cultural leadership, not as ideas, but as things I had to navigate in real time, across markets, through crises, inside boardrooms. It's also where I trained as a CPCC‑certified coach, because the more senior I got, the more I saw that the real work was rarely strategic. It was internal.

“I don’t give advice—I create a space for people to tap into their own wisdom and potential.” — Megha Bhalla

FAQ

A few questions people ask

Is executive coaching the same as therapy?

No, though the two can sit comfortably alongside each other. Therapy often looks back, to understand and heal. Coaching looks forward: we work with where you are now and who you're becoming next, in your leadership and in your life.

How long are sessions, and how often do we meet?

Sessions typically run 50 to 60 minutes. Within the Six-Session Journey, we usually meet every two to three weeks, which gives you room to actually live the work between conversations.

Do we meet virtually or in person?

Almost all of my coaching happens virtually, which suits the range of time zones and cities my clients work from. In-person sessions can sometimes be arranged by request.

How do I know if I'm ready for coaching?

If you're asking the question, you're probably closer than you think. Readiness tends to look like a quiet sense that something needs to shift, alongside a willingness to sit with that honestly. A discovery call is a low-pressure way to find out.
