About

Twenty‑five years in the room, now beside you in it.

I spent twenty‑five years building a career across banking and technology — leading teams through reorganizations, product launches, market shifts, and the quieter, harder work of becoming the kind of leader people actually want to follow. I know what it feels like to sit at the table and privately wonder if you're doing this right.

That's the work I do now, as a CPCC‑certified coach: I partner with executives and leaders who are capable and accomplished on paper, and still navigating something real underneath — a transition, a ceiling they can't quite name, a version of leadership that finally feels like their own.

My approach is warm but direct. I believe transformation happens not by adding another framework on top of who you are, but by clearing away what's in the way of who you already are becoming. We start from within — that's the work.
