About

You already know more than you think. The work is learning to trust it.

Most of the senior leaders who come to this work are not lacking capability. They are navigating something more internal: a transition without a clear map, a season of growth that has outpaced the identity they built to get here, or a quiet sense that the way they have been leading is no longer the whole story.

That terrain is familiar. Not from reading about it, but from living it across 25 years of senior leadership in banking, organisational development, and people strategy across the UAE, the Middle East, and Asia. The CPCC certification came later, but the understanding of what leaders actually face (inside boardrooms, through mergers, through the pressure of building something from nothing) came first.

What that background makes possible in a coaching relationship is a particular kind of presence: someone who understands the stakes, speaks the language, and has no interest in giving you the answer. Because the answers that last are always yours.

“I don’t give advice—I create a space for people to tap into their own wisdom and potential.” Megha Bhalla

The belief at the centre of this work is simple: you are already creative, resourceful, and whole. Coaching, done well, doesn't add anything to you. It clears the space for what's already there.

FAQ

A few questions people ask

Is executive coaching the same as therapy?

No, though the two can sit comfortably alongside each other. Therapy often looks back, to understand and heal. Coaching looks forward: we work with where you are now and who you're becoming next, in your leadership and in your life.

How long are sessions, and how often do we meet?

Sessions typically run 50 to 60 minutes. Within the Six-Session Journey, we usually meet every two to three weeks, which gives you room to actually live the work between conversations.

Do we meet virtually or in person?

Almost all of my coaching happens virtually, which suits the range of time zones and cities my clients work from. In-person sessions can sometimes be arranged by request.

How do I know if I'm ready for coaching?

If you're asking the question, you're probably closer than you think. Readiness tends to look like a quiet sense that something needs to shift, alongside a willingness to sit with that honestly. A discovery call is a low-pressure way to find out.
