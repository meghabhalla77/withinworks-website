# LinkedIn: Theme - Real connections stay forever (bonus draft, 2026-09-25, revised for less "I")

Generalized from a real memory Megha shared (former colleagues who left the
company still stay in touch). Revised to reduce repetitive first-person
framing shared across the theme posts.

---

Some of the people who have moved on to other companies still reach out. Not for a reference, not for a favor. Just to say that a part of who they are today, professionally, was shaped by those years working together.

That has stayed close more than almost anything else from that chapter. Titles change. Org charts get redrawn. But real connections, the ones built on trust, do not end when the role does.

That is the kind of relationship worth building in coaching too. Not one that closes when the engagement ends, but one that stays, long after the work itself is finished.
