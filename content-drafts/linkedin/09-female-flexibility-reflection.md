# LinkedIn: Theme - Trust and flexibility, especially for women (bonus draft, 2026-09-25, revised for less "I")

Generalized from a real memory Megha shared (flexibility policy for female
staff). Revised to reduce repetitive first-person framing shared across the
theme posts.

---

One of the clearest lessons from twenty-five years of leadership: flexibility should never be treated as a concession, especially for the women on a team.

Showing up fully for work and fully for the rest of life are not competing commitments. They are the same person, capable of doing both well, given the trust and the room to do it.

This shows up constantly in coaching senior women through their own transitions. So much of what holds people back is not a lack of capability. It is a workplace that never made room for the whole of who they are.
