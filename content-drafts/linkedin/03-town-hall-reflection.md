# LinkedIn: Theme - People work for people, not money (Week 3 draft, 2026-09-25, revised for less "I")

Generalized from a real memory Megha shared (COVID scale-down, town hall
where employees offered to work without salary). Revised to reduce
repetitive first-person framing shared across the theme posts.

---

Twenty-five years of leadership taught one truth that no strategy document ever did: people do not work for an organization, and they rarely work for money alone. They work for people.

That played out most clearly at the hardest moments, when a business was under real pressure and there was every reason for people to walk away. The ones who stayed, stayed because of who was standing next to them, not because of what was written in their contract.

It is the thing that matters most in coaching too. Understanding why someone is truly committed, or why they are quietly leaving, comes from looking at their relationship with the person leading them. That tells you more than any engagement survey ever will.
