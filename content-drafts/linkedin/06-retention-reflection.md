# LinkedIn: Theme - Long tenure comes from trust, not pay (Week 6 draft, 2026-09-25, revised for less "I")

Generalized from a real memory Megha shared (long staff tenure, flat/family
culture). Revised to reduce repetitive first-person framing shared across
the theme posts.

---

Long tenure, people staying for a decade or more, rarely comes down to the paycheck.

It comes from the environment. The leadership. Enough space to be trusted rather than managed. Room for people's own instincts and judgment to be respected instead of flattened into someone else's process. A culture where the most junior person and the most senior person are treated the same way.

That is the lesson worth carrying into coaching too. Retention is never really a policy. It is a byproduct of how people are treated, every single day, especially when no one is watching.
