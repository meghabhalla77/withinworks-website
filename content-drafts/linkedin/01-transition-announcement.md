After twenty-five years in senior leadership, including as a C-suite executive and co-founder building a business from the ground up, I am beginning a new chapter.

Those years were spent building organizations, the systems and structures that let a business scale. What stayed with me was never the structures themselves. The hard part was always the person navigating them: the leader learning to trust a team they used to run alone, the founder realizing the instincts that built it would not be the ones that scaled it, the executive stepping into a bigger mandate while quietly wondering if they had earned it.

I kept being drawn to that part, long before I had language for it.

I am a Certified Professional Co-Active Coach (CPCC) and a member of the International Coaching Federation (ICF).

Transformation happens from within. I do not believe coaching should ask you to leave the human part of you at the door, especially not at the executive level, where the stakes are highest and the permission to be uncertain is lowest.

That is what I bring: a rare vantage point, having stood on both sides, once as the executive living the decision, now as the coach helping you navigate it. The rigour and grit of twenty-five years spent building and leading through uncertainty, paired with the discipline to hold space rather than hand you a solution.
